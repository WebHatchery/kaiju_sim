# Project Review

## Project

**Name:** Kaiju Breeding Simulator

**One Sentence Pitch:** Raise monsters to fight in tourament, gradually focus on stronger monsters via bloodlines and other perks


# 1. First Impression
Spend 10-20 minutes using the project without making changes.
- Started with 2 despite picking a single starter
- couldn't see a buy new monster option
- couldn't see a way of earning money
- training was static and only cost gold, unlimited gold would mean easily maxed out stats, monsters should have stamina
- combat worked well in the arena
- save and load worked correctly

# 2. Story
Would I keep reading?
- this is an ergent gameplay game that will depend on the monsters raised and fights fought, in the first round having a victor o n the record looked good, this should follow simliar tracks to horse racing.

# 3. Does it Have Potential?
Ignoring its current state...
Could this become something great with more work?
- I think a good combat simulator would do well, the breeding could be the key part of the game.

# 4. Biggest Problems
- unlimited stamina for training, starting with 2 after picking 1

# 5. Best Parts
What already works well?
the combat in the arena worked, to give a hit by hit commentary on the fight

# 6. Next Goal
What is the **single most important milestone**?
- moving from a vertical slice to adding more depth in the game, the bones are there, now it needs enough content for people to want to play it.

# 7. Content Notes (Optional)
Would this make an interesting YouTube video or livestream?
atm no, maybe in the future if the battles could actually be monsters fighting and not just stats on the page.
