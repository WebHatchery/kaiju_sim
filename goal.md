# Kaiju Breeding Simulator UI/UX Goal

## Objective

Refine the MVP UI into a polished, readable indie strategy interface. Do not add major systems yet. Improve clarity, spacing, hierarchy, and atmosphere so the game feels like a classified kaiju evolution facility rather than a tool or spreadsheet.

The player should quickly understand:

- which kaiju is selected
- what matters right now
- what action to take next

## Design Principles

- Show primary information first. Hide secondary stats in detail views, hover panels, or expanded records.
- Give layouts more room. Use clear spacing between sections and one focal area per screen.
- Make hierarchy obvious: screen title, selected kaiju, primary action, key stats, then secondary details.
- Simplify navigation. Use concise sections: Laboratory, Roster, Training, Arena, Breeding, Records, Settings.
- Use fewer hard borders. Prefer spacing, subtle contrast, depth, and restrained glow accents.
- Make each kaiju feel valuable through larger art, cleaner stats, rarity cues, trait styling, lineage, and history.
- Add subtle game feel with ambient grid glow, scanlines, holographic flicker, status pulses, arena notices, incubator activity, and live notifications. Effects must stay readable.

## Screen Priorities

### Laboratory

Mission control. Show active kaiju, current activities, breeding queue, alerts, and progress summaries. Remove dashboard clutter.

### Roster

Kaiju archive. Support filters, sorting, generation markers, compact/expanded views, and clear rarity or trait emphasis.

### Kaiju Record

The emotional core. Give each creature a strong portrait, lineage view, battle history, mutation history, and timeline entries.

### Training

Make training feel physical and risky. Use program cards, estimated growth, fatigue/risk indicators, progress motion, and specialization themes.

### Arena

Make competition feel event-driven. Use banners, rankings, rivals, streaks, seasonal events, and arena modifiers.

### Battle Results

Highlight key moments: major damage, trait activations, turning points, and final outcomes.

### Breeding

The long-term centerpiece. Make breeding feel experimental and exciting, not like a calculator. Emphasize lineage trees, offspring predictions, mutation chances, inheritance visuals, egg rarity, and incubation queues.

### Leaderboard

If kept as its own view, add portraits, badges, streaks, generation counts, and seasonal labels. Otherwise fold it into Arena or Records.

## Art Direction

Keep the dark sci-fi mood, restrained blue/cyan palette, pixel typography, and clean layouts. Avoid excessive neon, rainbow UI, MMO clutter, mobile-game overload, and free-to-play casino styling.

Target: premium indie strategy game.

Player fantasy: "I am running a classified kaiju evolution facility."
